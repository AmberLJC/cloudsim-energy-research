| 35 | **NEW DIRECTION: Embodied Carbon Lifecycle Optimization (Direction #4 from brainstorm.md)** | **Research Statement:** Current cloud energy optimization research exclusively models *operational* carbon — the CO₂ emitted by electricity consumed during server operation. However, manufacturing a server emits 1–4 tonnes CO₂eq *before it ever turns on* (embodied carbon).
