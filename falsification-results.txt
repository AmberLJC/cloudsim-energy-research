  At PUE=1.2: 5.256 TWh  (5,256,000 kWh)
  At PUE=1.8: 7.884 TWh  (7,884,000 kWh)
  At PUE=1.5 (fixed): 6.570 TWh  (6,570,000 kWh)

Difference PUE=1.2 → PUE=1.8:
  Delta energy = 2,628,000 kWh/year
